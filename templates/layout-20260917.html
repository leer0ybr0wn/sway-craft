Sway London Private Event Hire

With almost 1000 capacity, Central London's most adaptable & welcoming venue.

Hold your Private Hire event at SWAY London – One venue, with endless possibilities.

Located in-between the Holborn and Covent Garden Tube Stations on fashionable Great Queen Street, SWAY London is a versatile multi-use event space with a capacity of up to 1,000 guests with the possibility of a 3AM licence.

SWAY is custom made to bring the most ambitious events, or even the most private, to life.

Whether you're planning to your event during the day, the evening or late into the night your Corporate Event, Product Launch, Private Party, Awards Ceremony, Birthday Party, Networking Event or Christmas Party can see our space transformed into your vision, however ambitious your dream maybe.

From Conferences, Late-night Parties, Intimate Gatherings, Large-scale Production or as a Film Location, we have the space, facilities and flexibility to make it happen.

With premium food and drink, state-of-the-art AV, multiple spaces and a team that delivers, SWAY can do it all.

SWAY Sports. London's Premium Sports Bar

Where the game meets the experience.

Located just off Kingsway, between the nearby Tube Stations of Holborn and Covent Garden, our premium sports bar brings together live sport, great food, expertly crafted drinks and an electric atmosphere.
